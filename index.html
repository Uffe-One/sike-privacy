Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

SIKE is a message display app. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data we collect

None. SIKE does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no server, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

SIKE stores the following data locally on your device using browser storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your typed messages
  • Session history (up to 30 sessions)
  • App preferences (theme, auto-cycle speed)
  • Pro unlock status
  • Tap count

You can clear this data at any time by deleting the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. SIKE never sees or stores your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to all transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third parties

SIKE does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporters.

Children

SIKE does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
